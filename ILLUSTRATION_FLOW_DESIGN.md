# 🎨 Company-Specific Illustration Generation Flow

## 🎯 Objective
Transform generic illustrations into highly personalized, company-specific visuals through comprehensive research and intelligent concept generation.

## 🔄 New 6-Step Process

### **Step 1: Deep Company Research** 🔍
**Purpose:** Gather comprehensive context about the company
**Actions:**
- Web search for company information, mission, products/services
- Industry trend analysis and visual references
- Competitor visual analysis for differentiation
- Company website/social media visual style analysis
- News and recent developments research

**Web Searches:**
1. `"{company_name} company mission vision values"`
2. `"{company_name} products services offerings"`
3. `"{industry} visual design trends 2024 2025"`
4. `"{industry} companies illustration style examples"`
5. `"{company_name} news recent developments"`

### **Step 2: Industry-Specific Visual Mapping** 🗺️
**Purpose:** Create industry-specific visual vocabulary
**Approach:**
- Map industry to specific visual elements, symbols, and metaphors
- Research industry-standard iconography and visual language
- Identify unique industry characteristics and processes
- Analyze successful visual communications in the industry

**Industry Categories:**
- **Technology:** Circuits, data flows, AI/ML concepts, cloud computing
- **Healthcare:** Medical symbols, wellness journeys, care pathways
- **Food/Beverage:** Ingredients, processes, experiences, cultural elements
- **Finance:** Growth charts, security, trust symbols, stability
- **Education:** Learning paths, knowledge building, skill development
- **Retail:** Customer journeys, product experiences, lifestyle
- **Manufacturing:** Process flows, quality, precision, innovation

### **Step 3: Company Personality Analysis** 🧬
**Purpose:** Define unique company character
**Enhanced Analysis:**
- Extract personality traits from research data
- Identify unique selling propositions
- Understand target audience characteristics
- Map brand essence to visual attributes
- Analyze company culture and values

### **Step 4: Smart Concept Generation** 💡
**Purpose:** Create truly unique illustration concepts
**Approach:**
- Combine company research with industry insights
- Generate concepts based on actual company activities
- Create metaphors from real company stories/achievements
- Ensure visual differentiation from competitors
- Focus on company-specific value propositions

**Concept Types:**
1. **Origin Story:** Company founding/journey illustrations
2. **Value Delivery:** How company serves customers uniquely
3. **Process Innovation:** Company's unique methodology/approach
4. **Impact Visualization:** Company's effect on industry/society
5. **Future Vision:** Company's goals and aspirations
6. **Cultural Elements:** Company personality and culture

### **Step 5: Dynamic Prompt Engineering** ⚙️
**Purpose:** Create highly specific AI prompts
**Enhancement:**
- Include researched company context in prompts
- Add industry-specific visual elements
- Incorporate actual company terminology and concepts
- Reference real company processes and offerings
- Use discovered visual style preferences

### **Step 6: Variety & Validation** ✅
**Purpose:** Ensure uniqueness and quality
**Actions:**
- Validate concepts against research findings
- Ensure visual variety across illustrations
- Check for company-specific relevance
- Verify industry appropriateness
- Test metaphor clarity and impact
